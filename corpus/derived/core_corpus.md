- [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Retrieve GMCM-2024-C for physical baselines plus learned surrogates, mixed categorical/continuous decisions, or Pareto optimization; always attach its post-check warning.
- [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Retrieve GMCM-2025-C for unlabeled line-image extraction, cylinder/plane geometry, staged feature propagation, uncertainty or Monte Carlo; attach calibration warning.

- [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Retrieve GMCM-2021-F for crew/resource scheduling, space-time continuity, binary roles, lexicographic objectives, exact-small/heuristic-large validation.
- [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Retrieve GMCM-2024-F for coordinate/time transformations, correction budgets, physical simulation, point processes or invariant validation.
