# Common Mistakes Seen Across the Corpus

1. **Algorithm stacking.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Several optimizers/classifiers are named even when the simple baseline is equal or nearly equal. [ANALYST INFERENCE] Add complexity only for material evidence gain.
2. **Missing baseline.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Packing, segmentation, stochastic simulation and control often lack a reproducible simple comparator.
3. **Single flattering metric.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] High R²/accuracy can coexist with leakage, imbalance, poor range errors or no calibration.
4. **Subjective parameters.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Clustering thresholds, AHP weights, traffic triggers, uncertainty mappings and capacity uplifts often lack sensitivity/support.
5. **Weak model explanation.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] A BiLSTM over unordered features or a GA for an algebraic structure needs more than popularity as rationale.
6. **Question-interface loss.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Sequential batching/packing and predictor/optimizer pipelines can discard global optimality or uncertainty.
7. **No post-optimization validation.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Surrogate optima, lane policies and camera/drilling layouts are presented without independent constraint/impact checks.
8. **Sensitivity theater.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] A single alternate step/noise level or many datasets is not systematic sensitivity/robustness.
9. **Many figures, little evidence.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Preprocessing galleries, heatmaps and one stochastic realization can visualize assumptions rather than validate them.
10. **Abstract/result mismatch.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] “Best,” “accurate” and exact delay/optimization numbers can disagree with tables/body.
11. **Causal overstatement.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Clinical treatment rankings and lane-opening effects arise from association or assumed counterfactuals, not identified causal effects.
12. **Feasible equals optimal.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] A converged heuristic schedule/layout remains best-found without a bound/certificate.
13. **Probability without calibration.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Normalized distances/angles are scores until tied to observed frequencies or a generative probability model.
14. **Leakage-prone splitting.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Adjacent times, repeated operating conditions, multiple patient visits or feature selection outside folds can cross train/test boundaries.
15. **Metric manipulation.** [CROSS-PAPER PATTERN] Swapping TP/TN because one class is scarce invalidates interpretation. Negative results must stay negative.

[ANALYST INFERENCE] “Excellent paper” is not an evidence category. Reuse a move only after the current data, assumptions and validation make it defensible.
